# 🏨 Hospitality Analytics – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI report for the Hospitality domain**, created to analyze hotel performance, revenue, and booking data.

The project aims to help **AtliQ Grands**, a prominent player in the hospitality industry, regain market share and revenue that have been declining, particularly within the luxury and business hotels category. As a data analyst, I developed a comprehensive Power BI report to provide data-driven insights and actionable recommendations to achieve this goal.

## 🔎 5 Key Discoveries from the Hospitality Analysis

### 👉 1. Performance Overview – Identified Overall Hotel Performance

The Performance Overview helped track key hospitality KPIs such as Revenue, RevPAR, ADR, Occupancy %, DSRN, DBRN, DURN, and Realisation %. Overall, AtliQ generated **₹1.69Bn revenue**, achieved **57.79% occupancy**, and recorded **70.14% realisation**, providing a clear view of the business's operational performance.

### 👉 2. Revenue Overview – Mumbai is the Strongest Revenue Market

The Revenue Overview showed that **Mumbai generated the highest revenue at ₹661M (39.14%)**, followed by Bangalore at ₹415M. This helps management identify high-performing cities and prioritize strategies for revenue growth.

### 👉 3. Revenue Overview – Luxury Hotels Drive Revenue

The **Luxury category contributed 61.62% of total revenue**, compared with 38.38% from Business hotels. This highlights the importance of the luxury segment and provides an opportunity to strengthen its offerings and maximize profitability.

### 👉 4. Booking Overview – High Cancellation Rate is a Concern

The Booking Overview revealed **133K total bookings**, of which approximately **33K were cancelled**, resulting in a **24.84% cancellation rate**. Reducing cancellations could help AtliQ improve realized revenue and operational efficiency.

### 👉 5. Booking Overview – Weekday Demand is Much Stronger

Weekday bookings generated approximately **₹1.16Bn in revenue**, more than twice the **₹0.52Bn** generated on weekends. This insight can help AtliQ optimize pricing, promotions, staffing, and room availability based on demand patterns.
